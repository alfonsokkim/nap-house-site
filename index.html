Nap House

Sleep well. Build your room.

  • Your sleep, scored each morning from Apple Health: good nights earn coins.
  • Spend them decorating a pixel-art room: furniture, pets, wallpapers and window views.
  • Live in a Nap House with friends, and grow it together from a rickety shed into a castle.
  • Private by default: only how long you sleep is kept, and your sleep graph is shared only if you choose.
