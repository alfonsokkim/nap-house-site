Nap House Privacy Policy

Updated 29 September 2026

Nap House turns a good night's sleep into coins you spend decorating a room, in a house you share with friends. This policy explains what the app keeps, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: we keep as little as possible, we never sell it, and there are no ads or tracking.

What we keep

  • Your account: a unique ID and the email address and name that Apple or Google gives us when you sign in (Apple may give a private relay address), plus the username and display name you choose. You can also play as a guest without signing in: the app then makes an anonymous account, with a unique ID and no email or name (only a display name, if you give one). Signing in with Apple or Google later adds it to that same account, so nothing is lost.
  • Your sleep: one number per night, the minutes you were asleep, with the date you woke up and the score and coins the app gives it. When you allow it, the app reads sleep analysis from Apple Health (or Health Connect on Android) to work this out. It reads nothing else from Apple Health: no heart rate, no sleep stages, no location, and it never writes to Apple Health. The name of the tracker you use is kept only so we can use the same source each night, and it's never shown to anyone else. If you don't use a sleep tracker, you can tell the app yourself instead: tap Good night and Good morning, or type in how long you slept. Either way, only the minutes are kept.
  • Your game: your coins, what you've bought, how your room is arranged, the houses you're in and the coins you've given them, and the invitations you create. Invitation links are stored only as a one-way fingerprint.

How we use it

Only to run Nap House: to sign you in, score your nights and pay your coins, save your room, and show your house to its members. We don't use your data, including your health data, for advertising, and we don't sell it or share it with data brokers. Health data from Apple Health is used only to provide these features.

Who can see it

  • People in your houses see your username and display name, your room, and what you've given to the house. They never see your coins, your email or your sleep, unless you turn on Share sleep graph. Then they see your nightly sleep length and scores (never your tracker). You can turn it off any time.
  • Nobody else. Houses are private and people join only with an invitation link or code from a member.
  • Service providers who run the app for us: Supabase hosts the database and sign-in (in Sydney, Australia), and Apple and Google handle signing in. They process data only on our instructions.

Keeping it safe

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). The database enforces who can read what for every request, and changes to coins, purchases and houses go through checked server functions only.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your sleep data in the app (Profile → Delete sleep data). Your room and coins stay.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). Everything of yours is deleted: account, sleep, room, coins and memberships. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID. Houses you were in carry on without you. Copies in our hosting provider's encrypted backups are removed as those backups roll over.
  • Signing out of a guest account deletes it, the same way, because nobody can sign back in to it. Sign in with Apple or Google first to keep it.
  • You can stop Nap House reading Apple Health at any time in the Health app (Sharing → Apps).
  • To ask what we hold about you, or for any privacy question, email us (below).

Children

Nap House isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date above. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app.
