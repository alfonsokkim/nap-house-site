Nap House Privacy Policy

Updated 30 September 2026

Nap House turns a good night's sleep into coins you spend decorating a room, in a house you share with friends. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, where it's kept, how long we keep it and how to have it deleted. In short: we keep as little as we can, we never sell it, and there are no ads or tracking.

Nap House is made by Alfonso Kim, an individual developer in Australia ("we", "us"). You can reach us at alfonso.kim27@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: a unique account ID and the email address Apple or Google gives us (Apple may give a private relay address). Google also sends the name on your Google account and a link to your profile photo, which our sign-in provider stores. If you start a new account with Google, that name is your display name until you choose your own; we never use the photo.
  • The username and display name you choose.
  • If you play as a guest: an anonymous account with a unique ID and no email or name (only a display name, if you give one). Signing in with Apple or Google later adds it to that same account, so nothing is lost.
  • When you created your account, when you last signed in, and which version of our terms you agreed to, and when.
  • If you signed in with Apple: a token from Apple, kept only so we can disconnect Nap House from your Apple ID when you delete your account.

Your sleep

  • One entry per night: the date you woke up, how many minutes you were asleep, the score and coins it earned, and whether it came from a tracker or you entered it yourself.
  • With your permission, the app reads sleep records from Apple Health (or Health Connect on Android): when you were in bed, awake or asleep (including sleep stages, if your tracker records them) and which app or device recorded them. It reads up to the last 30 days, works out on your phone how many minutes you were asleep each night, and sends us only those nightly totals. The times, stages and tracker details stay on your phone. It reads nothing else from Apple Health or Health Connect (no heart rate, no location), and it never writes to them.
  • If you don't use a sleep tracker, you can tell the app yourself: tap Good night and Good morning, or type in how long you slept. Your bedtime stays on your phone until you tap Good morning, and only the minutes are sent to us.

Your game

  • Your coins and coin history, what you've bought with them, how your room is arranged, the houses you're in and the ones you started, their names, what you've given them, the invitations you create (stored only as a one-way fingerprint) and your settings (such as Share sleep graph).
  • Reports you send us about other players (with the names they had then), reports other players send about you, and who you've blocked.

Technical information

  • When the app talks to our servers, our hosting provider records technical details such as your IP address, device type and app version, for security and to fix problems. Your sign-in sessions also keep the IP address and device they started on, until you sign out or delete your account.
  • When the app checks for updates, Expo (the service that delivers them) receives your IP address, your phone's operating system and a random ID for this installation of the app.

Kept only on your phone (never sent to us): your bedtime if you enter your sleep yourself, a night you entered while offline (until it's sent), an invitation you've opened but not used yet, and the picture of your room and last night's hours that the home-screen widget shows.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and we don't use analytics or advertising tools.

Your health information and your consent

Information about your sleep is health information, which Australian privacy law treats as sensitive information. We only collect it with your consent: when you connect Apple Health or Health Connect (the app first explains what it reads and where it goes, then your phone asks you), or when you enter your sleep yourself.

  • You can withdraw your consent at any time: turn off Nap House's access in the Health app (or in Settings, then Health, then Data Access & Devices), or in Health Connect's app permissions, or switch to entering your sleep yourself in Profile. To delete the sleep we already have, use Delete sleep data (see "Your choices and rights").
  • We use your sleep information only to score your nights, pay your coins and show your journal, and to show your housemates your sleep graph if you choose to share it. We never use it for advertising or marketing, never sell it, never give it to data brokers, and never store it in iCloud.
  • Nap House's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Nap House is a game, not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice.

How we use your information

  • To run Nap House: sign you in, score your nights and pay your coins, save your room, run your houses and invitations, and delete your data when you ask.
  • To keep Nap House safe and fair: stop cheating and abuse, check names, and handle reports.
  • To answer you when you contact us, and to meet our legal obligations.

Some of this is automatic. Our server scores each night and pays coins using the formula shown in the app (under How scoring works), and an automatic filter checks the names people choose. Decisions to suspend or close an account are made by a person.

Who can see it

  • People in your houses see your username and display name, your room and what you've given the house. They never see your coins, your email or your sleep, unless you turn on Share sleep graph: then everyone in your houses sees how long you slept and your scores for your last 14 nights (never your tracker). You can turn it off at any time. Someone you block no longer shows up in your houses for you.
  • People with an invitation to one of your houses see the house's name and how many people are in it.
  • Nobody else in the app. Houses are private, and guests can't be in one.
  • Our service providers, who run parts of Nap House for us (listed in the next section). They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Apple and Google, when you sign in with them. They handle this under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or someone's safety is at risk.
  • Whoever runs Nap House next, if Nap House moves to a company we set up or to someone who takes it over. Your information would stay under this policy, and we'd tell you first.

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

Where your information is kept

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in, in Tokyo, Japan. Supabase, Inc. is based in the United States, and it (and companies it uses) may access limited information from other countries, mainly the United States, to run and support the service.
  • Expo delivers app updates, from the United States.
  • GitHub hosts this website, in the United States.
  • Apple and Google provide sign-in, from the United States.

So your information is stored in Japan and may be accessed from the United States. We use providers that protect information, under agreements that require them to handle it properly.

Keeping it safe

  • Information travels encrypted (HTTPS), and our database is encrypted where it's stored.
  • The database checks who can read what on every request, and changes to coins, purchases and houses go through checked server functions only.
  • Only we can use the database's admin tools. We look at your information only when we need to (for example to fix a problem you report, or to check a report), and we don't read your sleep for any other reason.
  • If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while you have an account.
  • Delete sleep data: your nights, and the coin history that would reveal them, are deleted straight away.
  • Delete account: your account and everything linked to it are deleted from our database straight away. Copies in our hosting provider's encrypted backups and server logs are gone within 7 days. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Nap House's access to your Apple ID.
  • A house you started carries on without you if other people are still in it, and keeps the name you gave it.
  • Guest accounts are deleted when you sign out. A guest account you can no longer open (for example after deleting the app) stays on our servers until you ask us to delete it.
  • Reports are kept while we deal with them, and deleted within 12 months after.

Your choices and rights

  • See your information: ask us for a copy of what we hold about you. It's free, and we'll reply within 30 days.
  • Correct it: ask us to fix anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your sleep data: Profile, then Delete sleep data. Your room and coins stay.
  • Delete your account: Profile, then Delete account. Signing out of a guest account deletes it too.
  • Stop Nap House reading your sleep: see "Your health information and your consent".
  • Stop sharing your sleep graph: turn off Share sleep graph in Profile.

Deleting your account without the app

If you can't use the app, email alfonso.kim27@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Nap House account", from the email address linked to your Apple or Google sign-in (or tell us your username). We'll check it's your account, delete it and everything linked to it within 30 days, and email you when it's done. "How long we keep it" says what's deleted and when.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please email us. We'll reply within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992). If you live outside Australia, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

Nap House is for people 13 and older, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

If you're 13 to 17: your sleep graph is private unless you choose to share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date above. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app first.
