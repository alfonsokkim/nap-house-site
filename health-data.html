Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Updated 30 September 2026

This page is for people in Washington State and Nevada, whose laws (Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's SB 370) give extra rights over "consumer health data". It adds to our Privacy Policy, which covers everything else. Nap House is made by Alfonso Kim ("we", "us"), reachable at alfonso.kim27@gmail.com.

The consumer health data we collect

  • How many minutes you slept each night, and the date you woke up.
  • The Night Score and coins each night earned, and the streaks worked out from your nights.
  • Whether a night came from a sleep tracker or you entered it yourself.

Nothing else about your health: no sleep stages, heart rate, location or which tracker you use leaves your phone.

Where it comes from

  • From you, when you tap Good night and Good morning or type in how long you slept.
  • From Apple Health or Health Connect on your phone, when you allow it. The app explains what it reads first, then your phone asks you.

Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the features you ask for: scoring your nights, paying your coins, showing your sleep journal, and, if you turn on Share sleep graph, showing your recent nights to the people in your houses. We never use it for advertising or marketing, and we don't use geofencing.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, the company that hosts our database, stores it for us (in Tokyo, Japan) under a contract that lets it use the data only to provide that service.
  • The people in your houses, only if you turn on Share sleep graph. The app asks you first, and you can turn it off at any time.

We don't sell consumer health data, and we don't share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Your rights

  • Know and access: ask whether we collect or share your consumer health data, and get a copy, with a list of everyone we've shared it with and how to contact them.
  • Delete: in the app (Profile, then Delete sleep data, or Delete account), or by emailing us. Deletion includes our hosting provider's backups, which roll over within 7 days.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Nap House's access in the Health app or Health Connect, turn off Share sleep graph, or switch to entering your sleep yourself.

To use these rights, email alfonso.kim27@gmail.com from the email address linked to your sign-in (or tell us your username). We'll respond within 45 days. If we turn down your request, reply with "Appeal" and we'll look at it again within 45 days and explain our decision. If you're still unhappy, you can contact your state's Attorney General (Washington: atg.wa.gov; Nevada: ag.nv.gov).

We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.
